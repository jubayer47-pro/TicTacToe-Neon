Tic Tac Toe Neon

Welcome to Tic Tac Toe Neon, a modern mobile-friendly version of the classic Tic Tac Toe game.

🎮 Game Modes

Play against the AI with Easy, Medium and Hard difficulty, or challenge a friend in Player vs Player mode.

🤖 Smart AI

The game includes multiple AI difficulty levels. Hard mode uses a strategic decision-making system to provide a serious challenge.

🏆 Player Statistics

Your games, wins, losses and draws are tracked locally on your device so you can follow your progress.

🎯 Daily Challenge

Every day you can challenge yourself by trying to defeat the Hard AI.

🎨 Themes

Choose from Purple, Blue, Green and Red themes. Your selected theme is saved automatically.

🔊 Sound & Vibration

You can independently turn game sound and vibration ON or OFF from Settings.

📱 Mobile Friendly

The interface is optimized for smartphones and touch-screen gameplay.

💾 Local Data

Basic game statistics and preferences are stored locally on your device.

❤️ Designed for Fun

Tic Tac Toe Neon combines the simplicity of the classic game with a colorful modern interface.

Version: 2.0

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Personal Information

Tic Tac Toe Neon does not require an account or ask players to provide personal information such as a phone number, password or email address for normal gameplay.

2. Game Statistics

The game may store basic statistics such as games played, wins, losses and draws. These statistics are used only to provide the Player Stats feature.

3. Local Storage

Game statistics, selected theme, language and sound/vibration preferences may be stored locally on the device.

4. Internet Connection

Normal gameplay is designed to work offline. Some external functions, such as sharing, may require an internet connection.

5. Camera

The core game does not require camera access.

6. Microphone

The core game does not require microphone access.

7. Location

The game does not require location information for normal gameplay.

8. Third-Party Services

The basic game does not intentionally send gameplay statistics to a remote server.

9. Data Security

Basic game data stored locally remains associated with the browser or application storage on the device.

10. Data Deletion

You can reset saved game statistics using the Reset Statistics option. Clearing application or browser storage may also remove saved preferences.

11. Children's Privacy

The game does not intentionally request personal information from players.

12. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features or services change.
